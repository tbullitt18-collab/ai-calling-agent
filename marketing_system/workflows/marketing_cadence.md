# Daily & Weekly Marketing Cadence

This document outlines the operational rhythm for the Rain Check AI Marketing System.

---

## Daily Operations (Micro-Optimizations)

**Time:** 9:00 AM (or automated trigger)
**Lead:** Analytics Agent

1. **Budget Check:** Analytics Agent reviews yesterday's spend to ensure campaigns are pacing correctly and not burning cash on anomaly clicks.
2. **Loser Harvesting:** Immediately pause any ads that have spent > 1.5x Target CPA with zero conversions. 
3. **Alerting:** If CPA spikes > 20% compared to the 7-day average, send an alert to the Strategy Agent to investigate.

---

## Weekly Operations (Strategic Shifts)

**Time:** Monday, 10:00 AM
**Lead:** Strategy Agent

1. **Ingest Research (Research Agent):**
   - Review new long-tail keywords or competitor weaknesses identified over the weekend. 
2. **Review Analytics (Analytics Agent):**
   - Read the weekly Performance Report.
   - Solidify budget allocation for the current week (e.g., Shift 20% budget from Meta to Google).
3. **Brief Generation:**
   - Draft a new Campaign Brief based on winning personas or identified gaps.
4. **Creative Handoff (Creative Agent):**
   - By Monday 3:00 PM, Creative Agent delivers all requested variant copy, Hooks, and Visual Directives.
5. **Launch:**
   - Implement new creative/keywords in the ad platforms.

---

## Monthly Review (The Big Picture)

1. **Persona Audit:** Are we still targeting the right people? (e.g., Do Salon owners churn faster than HVAC owners?). Handled by Research Agent + Analytics Agent.
2. **Positioning Update:** Update `personas_and_positioning.md` if a new, wildly successful angle is discovered.
