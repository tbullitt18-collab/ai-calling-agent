Core Features

Why Rain Check?

A fully-autonomous phone assistant built to protect your peace of mind. Let the AI do the talking.

Hyper-Realistic Voice

State-of-the-art TTS clones your voice perfectly. The person on the other end won't know they're talking to an AI agent.

🧠

Contextual Intelligence

Powered by Google Gemini 2.0. Your twin dynamically responds to follow-up questions, deflects hostility, and ends calls politely.

Delayed Scheduling

Don't want to wake up at 6 AM to call in? Schedule the time and go back to sleep. Your AI twin handles it.

Privacy First

Audio and transcripts are processed securely. Voice clones remain strictly localized to your authenticated session.

✏️

Editable Voice Profiles

Fine-tune your voice clones at any time. Rename, update characteristics, or swap active profiles from the dashboard.

👤

Personalized Setup

Configure your employee ID, shift schedule, and workplace details once — your AI uses them to sound completely authentic.
