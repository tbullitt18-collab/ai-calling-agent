Privacy & Security Policy

At Rain Check, your privacy and data security are our highest priorities. This IBM hackathon prototype is built with a security-first architecture.

Data Handling & Storage

  • Voice Cloning: Voice models are securely generated via ElevenLabs API. We do not persist raw audio uploads after the cloning process completes.
  • Knowledge Base: Your FAQs, schedule, and contacts are stored securely in MongoDB Atlas and only accessed when you schedule a call.
  • Call Audio: Live call audio streams securely via Vonage WebSockets and Google Cloud Speech-to-Text. We do not record or retain the audio of your calls.

AI Processing

Your context data is processed by Google Vertex AI (Gemini) solely for the purpose of navigating your phone call. Your personal data is never used to train Google's foundation models.

Consent & Deletion

You retain full control over your data. Deleting your voice profile or knowledge base items removes them from our systems and third-party APIs immediately.
