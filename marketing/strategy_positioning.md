# Strategy Memo: Initial Positioning & Focus

Based on the research profiles of hourly workers and their call-out anxiety, here is the strategic direction for Rain Check's initial marketing push.

---

## 1. Core Positioning Statements (The Angles)

We will test three distinct angles to see which resonates the most and drives the cheapest installs.

*   **Angle A: The Ultimate Anxiety Relief (Focus on feelings)**
    *   *Statement:* "Rain Check is the app that makes the worst phone call of your day disappear. Just tap a button, and we’ll professionally notify your manager so you can skip the panic and just rest."
*   **Angle B: The Boundary Setter (Focus on professionalism)**
    *   *Statement:* "You earned your sick days; you shouldn't have to defend them. Rain Check professionally notifies your job that you're out, ending the back-and-forth guilt trips and keeping your boundaries firm."
*   **Angle C: The Job Protector (Focus on safety/policy)**
    *   *Statement:* "Never risk a no-call, no-show again. If you're too burned out or sick to face your boss, Rain Check handles the call precisely to policy, generating proof that you notified them."

---

## 2. Google Search Keyword Themes
*Google Ads targets users with high, immediate intent (e.g., they need to call out right now).*

**Theme 1: Direct "How-To" Intent**
*   *Keywords:* "how to call in sick to work", "how to call off a shift", "what to say when calling in sick", "calling in sick text examples"

**Theme 2: Anxiety/Fear Intent**
*   *Keywords:* "calling in sick anxiety", "scared to call boss sick", "phone anxiety calling out of work"

**Theme 3: Job/Industry Specific**
*   *Keywords:* "how to call in sick retail", "calling off shift fast food", "how to call out of work walmart"

---

## 3. TikTok / Instagram Reel Hook Angles
*Social video targets passive scrollers, often late at night before a dreaded morning shift.*

**Hook 1: High Relatability (POV Style)**
*   *Angle:* "POV: It's 5:00 AM, you're sick, and you're rehearsing what to say to your manager in the mirror for the 10th time."

**Hook 2: The Action-Reaction (Relief)**
*   *Angle:* "The feeling when you successfully call out of work and can finally go back to sleep..." (Transitions into how Rain Check does it for you).

**Hook 3: The Educational "Hack"**
*   *Angle:* "Are you still calling your boss when you're sick? Stop doing that. There's an app that literally does the awkward phone call for you so they can't guilt-trip you into coming in."

**Hook 4: Storytime / Toxic Boss**
*   *Angle:* "Storytime: How I avoided getting yelled at by my toxic manager when I needed a mental health day..."
