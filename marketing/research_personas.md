# Research Output: Ideal Customer Personas & Pain Points

Based on market analysis of shift-working demographics and social media discussion (primarily Reddit and forums regarding retail/fast-food work and phone anxiety), here are the initial target personas and pain points for Rain Check.

---

## 1. Persona: The Anxious Barista (Gen-Z) ☕
*   **Age:** 19 – 25
*   **Role:** Retail, Coffee Shop, or Fast Food Worker
*   **Financial Situation:** Living paycheck to paycheck or supporting themselves through school.
*   **Psychographics:** Heavily affected by social/phone anxiety. Deeply fears confrontation or disappointing figures of authority (their manager). 
*   **The Main Problem:** They feel physically ill or mentally burned out, but the thought of calling their manager and hearing the manager sound annoyed or skeptical induces massive panic. 
*   **Current Solution:** They often text (which breaks policy), try to find a coworker to cover their shift via group chat for hours while sick, or just "power through" and go to work sick.

## 2. Persona: The Burned-Out Warehouse Worker (Millennial) 📦
*   **Age:** 28 – 40
*   **Role:** Amazon Warehouse, Factory, or Delivery Driver
*   **Financial Situation:** Reliant on their job to pay rent/mortgage. Cannot afford to lose the job, but works grueling, physically demanding shifts.
*   **Psychographics:** Fed up with "toxic productivity." They are overworked, often physically injured or fatigued, and deeply resent companies that make it difficult to use their earned sick-leave.
*   **The Main Problem:** Their employer has a strict, convoluted, or frustrating "call in" procedure. The managers are notoriously unsympathetic or penalize workers. They want a bulletproof, professional way to say "I'm not coming in" without getting into a debate with dispatch or their supervisor.
*   **Current Solution:** They call, endure the guilt trip, or sometimes risk a "no-call, no-show" if their burnout is severe enough.

---

## 3. Core Pain Points Rain Check Solves
1.  **The "Band-Aid" Hurdle:** The anticipation of dialing the number is the most anxiety-inducing part of calling out. Rain Check rips the band-aid off digitally.
2.  **The Guilt Trip:** Managers often intentionally sound disappointed or ask probing questions to pressure the employee to come in. Rain Check eliminates the back-and-forth debate.
3.  **The "Powering Through" Conditioning:** People feel they aren't "sick enough" to call in. Rain Check makes the transaction feel like an administrative task rather than a personal failure.
4.  **Proof of Notification:** In toxic jobs, managers sometimes claim "I never got your call." Rain Check provides an objective log of the communication.
