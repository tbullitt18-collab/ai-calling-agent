# Rain Check Workflow Checklists

Follow these checklists to run your Rain Check marketing system using less than 30-60 minutes of your time. This assumes your AI agents are already primed and doing the heavy lifting.

---

## Daily Workflow (10–30 Minutes)

1. **Review Analytics Report**
   *   [ ] Open the Daily Summary from `RainCheck_Analytics`.
   *   [ ] Review key metrics (Spend, CPA, Installs) for active campaigns across Google Ads and TikTok/IG.
   *   [ ] Review the "Best" and "Worst" performing ads/campaigns.
2. **Execute Action Items**
   *   [ ] Open Google Ads / TikTok Ads Manager in your browser.
   *   [ ] Execute the 3-5 specific recommendations from the Analytics report (e.g., Pause underperforming ad group, increase budget on winning campaign).
3. **Approve & Launch New Creatives**
   *   [ ] Review the ready-to-use ad copy / scripts generated by `RainCheck_Creative` for the day's tests.
   *   [ ] Lightly edit if necessary.
   *   [ ] Paste the new copy into your ad accounts or record the UGC-style video using the provided script.
4. **End of Day**
   *   [ ] Close dashboard. You're done.

---

## Weekly Workflow (30–60 Minutes)

1. **Review Weekly Strategy Memo**
   *   [ ] Read the weekly memo from the `Strategy` and `Research` agents.
   *   [ ] Understand the updated audience insights (e.g., *Is the "anxiety" angle beating the "professionalism" angle?*)
   *   [ ] Review the recommendations for next week's experiments and new channels to test.
2. **Batch Creative Approval**
   *   [ ] Review the batch of creatives (search ads, scripts, organic posts) prepared by `RainCheck_Creative` for the upcoming week.
   *   [ ] Approve the batch. 
   *   [ ] (Optional) Pre-schedule organic posts in your social media scheduling tool.
3. **Budget & System Review**
   *   [ ] Check total aggregate spend for the week to ensure the limited budget ($5-$20/day) is being respected.
   *   [ ] Provide any high-level feedback or new product updates to the agent team so they can adjust their context for the next week.
